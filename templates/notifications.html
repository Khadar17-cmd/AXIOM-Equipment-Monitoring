DELIVERY RULES

When emails are sent

!
Warning condition

Triggered when simulated temperature or vibration crosses the warning threshold.

⌁
Critical condition

Triggered when readings enter the critical range or a fault is simulated.

↻
Duplicate protection

Repeated alerts for the same machine and status are limited to one email per 30 minutes.

SMTP setup required

For actual delivery, configure SMTP_HOST, SMTP_PORT, SMTP_USER, SMTP_PASSWORD and SMTP_FROM as environment variables before starting Flask. Until configured, alerts are recorded but emails are not sent.
